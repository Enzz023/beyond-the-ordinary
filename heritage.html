A Province Rich in History

Bataan is known for its important role in Philippine history, especially during World War II. Its landmarks and traditions remind us of the courage and sacrifices of the people who came before us.

The Fall of Bataan

The Fall of Bataan took place on April 9, 1942, during World War II. It marked the surrender of Filipino and American forces defending the Bataan Peninsula.

Historical Landmarks

Mount Samat National Shrine

This memorial honors the bravery and sacrifices of the soldiers who fought during the Battle of Bataan.

Zero Kilometer Death March Marker

Located in Mariveles, this marker commemorates the starting point of the Bataan Death March.

Preserving Our Heritage

Learning about Bataan's history helps preserve its cultural identity and reminds future generations of its important place in Philippine history.
